# Skooby.app Launch Email Campaign

## Goal
Drive qualified traffic to https://skooby.app and convert visitors into free accounts, then Pro upgrades.

## Audience
Use only opted-in subscribers or existing users who have agreed to receive Skooby updates. Do not upload scraped or purchased lists.

## Subject line tests
A. Stop guessing. Follow the crypto trail with Skooby.
B. Your wallet investigation command center is live 🔎
C. Meet Skooby: crypto tracking without the noise
D. Follow the money. Keep the evidence.
E. Skooby.app is on the case 🕵️‍♂️

## Preview text
Track public wallets, map transactions, save casebooks, learn on-chain patterns, and start free.

## Primary CTA
Start Free → https://skooby.app/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=launch&utm_campaign=skooby_launch

## Secondary CTA
Explore Skooby Pro → https://skooby.app/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=launch&utm_campaign=skooby_pro

## Recommended send sequence
1. Launch email — product promise + Start Free.
2. 48 hours later — use-case email: "Follow one wallet from clue to context."
3. 4 days later — education email: Bitcoin address tracking + Ethereum gas calculator.
4. 7 days later — Pro conversion email for engaged non-subscribers.

## UX direction
The HTML template uses a dark cinematic card, neon teal/lime accents, faux depth, layered gradients, high-contrast CTA buttons, and mobile-safe table-based email structure. This creates a 4D-style visual feel while staying compatible with major email clients.

## Compliance
- Keep a visible unsubscribe control.
- Use truthful claims only.
- Send only to recipients with an appropriate relationship/opt-in.
- Include sender identity and physical mailing address through the email platform footer settings.
